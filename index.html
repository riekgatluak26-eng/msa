Who We Are

A South Sudanese organisation, rooted in the places we serve.

Mission for Sustainable Advancement (MSA) is a legally registered civil society organization working across South Sudan. We started with a simple conviction: communities already hold the answers to their own challenges , what they often lack is the support, tools and partnerships to put those answers into practice.

Our teams are based in Juba, Maridi, Aweil and Yei, which means we are close to the families we work with , not visiting from a distance. From these offices we run programmes that reach displaced families, women, young people and children with practical, community-led support.

We don't pretend to have every answer. So we build alongside partners like Mercy Corps, the European Union and local youth networks , combining their resources and expertise with the knowledge our own staff and communities bring to the table.

"The people closest to the problem are the ones closest to the solution."

Our Vision

A South Sudan where every community has the capacity, dignity and opportunity to shape its own sustainable future.

Our Mission

To strengthen civil society, empower vulnerable communities, and promote peace, inclusion and sustainable development across South Sudan.

What We Stand For

Our values in practice.

Three principles guide every programme we run and every partnership we build.

01

Partnership First

We collaborate with communities, local authorities and international partners. No one organisation can do this alone.

02

Community-Led

Programmes are designed with the people they're for — not delivered to them. Local ownership is what makes change last.

03

Accountable

We are legally registered, transparent in our reporting, and answerable to the communities and partners who trust us.

Our Approach

Lasting change happens when communities lead it.

We work through collaborative partnerships — with communities, local authorities, CBOs, youth networks and international partners — to design programmes that are inclusive, accountable and locally owned. Our teams don't arrive with fixed answers. They sit down, listen, and build with the people who will carry the work forward long after we leave.
