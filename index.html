About MSA

Building a Literate, Healthy &
Well-Informed South Sudan

MSA (Mission for Sustainable Advancement) is a legally registered National Organisation operating in South Sudan. MSA began in 2000 operating as Maridi Students Association, transformed into Maridi Service Agency in 2005, and in 2021 — due to expansion across sectors — became Mission For Sustainable Advancement, maintaining its brand name MSA.

Through intense capacity building from external partners, MSA now operates with strengthened Governance, Management, Financial Management, Service Delivery and Human Resource Management — guided by functional financial, personnel, editorial, governance, volunteers and appraisal policies.

Our Mission

Empowering communities and Local Governments in South Sudan through Education, Health, Resilience and Recovery, Governance, Protection and media Programs for a sustainable future.

Our Vision

MSA envisions literate, healthy and well informed communities in South Sudan.

Featured Initiative

Promoting
Girl Child Education

MSA carries out massive community education awareness programs to promote education in the areas of our operation. In 2018, we conducted an in-school awareness campaign using women role models to talk to girls about the importance of education and the dangers of early marriage and unwanted pregnancy — under the theme "Girls Decide Today and Save Your Future."

In-School Awareness Campaigns

National Girls Education Day

MSA supports Government-led celebrations to amplify awareness on girls' education.

Key Achievements

Our Track Record

Milestones from the last five years across our programme areas.

Education

  • Established Maridi High School (mixed secondary)
  • 2 community Nursery Schools for IDPs
  • Girls' scholarships (S1–S4)
  • National Girls Education Day support

HIV/AIDS & SRH

  • VCT services since 2013 (youth-friendly)
  • School HIV/AIDS & SRHR clubs
  • Condom distribution at youth centres
  • Referrals to ART clinic

Child Protection

  • 17 LRA abducted children reunified
  • Trained child protection committees
  • Police trained on Child Act 2008
  • Pre-school for displaced children

Gender & GBV

  • Special Protection Unit at Maridi Police
  • ~20 GBV cases supported monthly
  • Radio awareness reaching 20,000+
  • Advocacy for 1st class judge (2017)

Livelihoods

  • Computer skills training for youth
  • Tailoring, mechanics & driving
  • Women's business start-up capital
  • Youth agriculture & sports programs

Information & Media

  • Maridi FM 88.9 established
  • Only privately operated community radio
  • Policy advocacy & sensitisation
  • Media house capacity strengthening
